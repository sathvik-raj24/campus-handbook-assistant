FICTIONAL CAMPUS LIBRARY POLICY FOR TEACHING ONLY
This document contains invented rules for the workshop. It does not describe VCET or any real institution.

Third year undergraduate students may borrow four library books at a time. Each book may be borrowed for fourteen days. A student must present a valid student identity card at the issue desk. Reference books and journals may be read in the library but cannot be borrowed. Library hours are 8 AM to 6 PM on weekdays.

A borrowed book may be renewed once for seven additional days if no other student has reserved it. Renewal must be requested before the due date. Overdue books incur a fine of two rupees per book per day. A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or settled with the librarian. For library questions, contact the library help desk on the ground floor of Block B.
